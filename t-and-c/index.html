Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Your privacy is important to Ultimate Refunds. We are committed to handling your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Personal Information We Collect

We collect personal information necessary to provide our unclaimed money recovery services. This may include:

  • Full name, date of birth and previous names
  • Current and previous residential or business addresses
  • Email address, phone number and preferred contact method
  • Tax File Number (TFN) where required by a holding authority
  • ABN or ACN (for business claimants)
  • Bank account details for disbursement of recovered funds
  • Identity documents (passport, driver's licence, Medicare card etc.)
  • Information about deceased estates where applicable

2. How We Use Your Personal Information

  • Investigate and recover unclaimed money or assets on your behalf
  • Verify your identity with relevant government authorities and holding bodies
  • Lodge claims with the relevant state or federal authority
  • Communicate with you about the progress of your claim
  • Process disbursement of recovered funds to your nominated account
  • Comply with our legal obligations and improve our services

3. Automated Decision-Making and AI Systems

Ultimate Refunds currently uses and may in the future use automated systems and artificial intelligence (AI) tools to assist with:

  • Lead filtering and prioritisation: Enquiries submitted via our website or received by phone or email may be assessed and prioritised using automated tools.
  • Communication screening: Incoming emails and calls may be processed through AI-assisted systems to categorise, route or summarise communications for our team.
  • Document processing: Submitted identity documents and claim forms may be processed using automated tools to assist with verification and data extraction.

These systems assist our team but do not make final decisions about your claim. All substantive decisions are made by a human representative. You have the right to request human review of any decision by contacting admin@ultimaterefunds.com.au.

4. Disclosure of Your Personal Information

We may disclose your personal information to government authorities and holding bodies (e.g. Queensland Public Trustee, ASIC, ATO), our CRM and software providers (including Zoho Corporation), our legal advisors, and law enforcement where required by law. We will not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

5. Overseas Disclosure

Some service providers, including Zoho, may store or process your data on servers located overseas (including the United States and India). We take reasonable steps to ensure these providers comply with standards equivalent to Australian privacy law. By using our services, you consent to this disclosure.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Once your matter is concluded and retention obligations are met, your information will be securely destroyed or de-identified.

7. Securing Your Data

Ultimate Refunds takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access or disclosure. In the event of a data breach likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the OAIC and affected individuals in accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

8. Your Rights

Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), you have the right to access and correct your personal information, request human review of any automated decision, and make a complaint about how we have handled your information.

9. Complaints

If you have a complaint, please contact us first — we will respond within 30 days. If unsatisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or call 1300 363 992.
