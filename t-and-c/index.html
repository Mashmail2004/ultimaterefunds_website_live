Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to Ultimate Refunds. We are committed to handling your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, disclose and protect your personal information, and outlines your rights.

Last updated: April 2026

1. PERSONAL INFORMATION WE COLLECT

We collect personal information necessary to provide our unclaimed money recovery services. This may include:

  • Full name, date of birth and previous names
  • Current and previous residential or business addresses
  • Email address, phone number and preferred contact method
  • Tax File Number (TFN) where required by a holding authority
  • ABN or ACN (for business claimants)
  • Bank account details for disbursement of recovered funds
  • Identity documents (passport, driver's licence, Medicare card etc.)
  • Information about deceased estates where applicable

We collect this information directly from you via our website contact form, the Authority to Investigate & Release form, email, phone or post.

2. HOW WE USE YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

We use your personal information to:

  • Investigate and recover unclaimed money or assets on your behalf
  • Verify your identity with relevant government authorities and holding bodies
  • Lodge claims with the relevant state or federal authority
  • Communicate with you about the progress of your claim
  • Process disbursement of recovered funds to your nominated account
  • Comply with our legal obligations
  • Improve our services
3. AUTOMATED DECISION-MAKING AND AI SYSTEMS

Ultimate Refunds currently uses and may in the future use automated systems and artificial intelligence (AI) tools to assist with the following functions:

  • Lead filtering and prioritisation: Enquiries submitted via our website or received by phone or email may be assessed and prioritised using automated tools to determine follow-up urgency.
  • Communication screening: Incoming emails and calls may be processed through AI-assisted systems to categorise, route or summarise communications for our team.
  • Document processing: Submitted identity documents and claim forms may be processed using automated tools to assist with verification and data extraction.

These automated systems assist our team but do not make final decisions about your claim. All substantive decisions regarding your unclaimed money recovery are made by a human representative of Ultimate Refunds. You have the right to request human review of any decision that affects you by contacting us at admin@ultimaterefunds.com.au.

In compliance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and anticipated reforms effective December 2026, we will disclose all automated decision-making systems that use your personal information in this policy as they are introduced.

4. DISCLOSURE OF YOUR PERSONAL INFORMATION

We may disclose your personal information to:

  • Government authorities and holding bodies (e.g. Queensland Public Trustee, ASIC, ATO, state revenue offices) as required to lodge your claim
  • Our CRM and business software providers (including Zoho Corporation) for the purpose of managing your enquiry and claim — note that Zoho may store data on servers located outside Australia, including in the United States and India. We take reasonable steps to ensure these providers comply with standards equivalent to the APPs
  • Our legal advisors or accountants where necessary
  • Law enforcement or regulatory bodies where required by law

We will not sell, rent or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

5. OVERSEAS DISCLOSURE

Some of our service providers, including our CRM platform (Zoho), may store or process your data on servers located overseas (including the United States and India). We take reasonable steps to ensure that overseas recipients handle your information in a manner consistent with Australian privacy law. By using our services, you consent to this disclosure.

6. DATA RETENTION

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. Once your matter is concluded and retention obligations are met, your personal information will be securely destroyed or de-identified.

7. SECURING YOUR DATA

Ultimate Refunds takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information from loss, misuse, unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or destruction. These measures include secure email systems, password-protected file storage and restricted staff access.

In the event of a data breach that is likely to cause serious harm, we will notify the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) and affected individuals in accordance with the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

8. YOUR RIGHTS

Under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth), you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated information
  • Request human review of any automated decision that affects you
  • Make a complaint about how we have handled your personal information
9. COMPLAINTS

If you have a complaint about how Ultimate Refunds has handled your personal information, please contact us in the first instance. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at www.oaic.gov.au or by calling 1300 363 992.
